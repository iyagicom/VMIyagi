# Privacy Policy for VMIyagi

**Last updated: October 21, 2026**

## Overview
VMIyagi is a virtual machine manager for testing packages.

## Data Collection
VMIyagi does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer. VM images, snapshots and settings stay on your own PC. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

## Network Use
If you choose to download an operating system image, the app connects to the download address you selected.
